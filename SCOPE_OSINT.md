# Aether AI — Intelligence scope (hard boundary)

## What we build toward

Phase-1 was **open-source packaging** of public multi-angle collection (e.g. coastal / maritime OSINT reports with evidence + so-what).

Phase-2 (this track) adds **think-tank tradecraft on open sources**:

| Capability | Method |
|------------|--------|
| Multi-source collection | Exa + multi-angle queries (public web) |
| Competing hypotheses | ACH-style weights + falsifiers |
| Intention assessment | Most-likely intent + alternatives + conf |
| Forecasting | 7d / 30d / 90d / 12m base·upside·downside |
| Indicators & warnings | Watchlist + kill-criteria |
| Continuous train | Multi-part gym → G1+ strategic scenarios |

**Accuracy target:** match **public** analytic standards used by major open think tanks (structured judgments, calibrated confidence, falsifiers) — **not** a claim of classified ground-truth.

## What we will NOT do

- Classified systems access (CIA, ISRO, or any agency secret stack)
- Illegal intrusion, exfiltration, or “secret feed” hacks
- Pretending to be an official intelligence agency product
- Image/voice/video generation

## Lawful collaboration pattern

Agencies and research orgs can use Aether **only** as:

1. **Open-source analyst workstation** (public data → structured brief)
2. **Fusion helper** for material the human already has authority to use
3. **Training gym** for analytic quality (hypotheses, forecasts, indicators)

Human remains accountable for classification, authorities, and decisions.

## Modes

- `/osint` — strategic OSINT brief (auto strategic layer)
- `/strategic` — full ACH + intent + forecast brief
- `/forecast` — intention + horizon forecasts emphasis
- `/research` — general multi-angle research
